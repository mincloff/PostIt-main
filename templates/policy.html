Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

1. Information We Collect

PostIt collects information to provide and improve our social media management services. We collect:

  • Account Information: Email, name, and profile information when you create an account
  • Social Media Accounts: Access tokens and permissions for connected social platforms
  • Content Data: Posts, images, and content you create or schedule through our platform
  • Usage Analytics: How you interact with our platform and performance metrics
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, and device identifiers

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide social media management and posting services
  • Connect and manage your social media accounts
  • Generate analytics and performance reports
  • Improve our platform and develop new features
  • Communicate with you about your account and our services
  • Ensure platform security and prevent fraud

3. Social Media Integration

Important: When you connect social media accounts, we only access the permissions you explicitly grant. We never post without your authorization.

For connected social platforms, we may:

  • Read your profile information and follower counts
  • Post content on your behalf when you schedule or publish posts
  • Access analytics data to provide performance insights
  • Retrieve your existing posts for content analysis (if permitted)

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Third-party services that help us operate our platform
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with mergers or acquisitions
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and monitoring
  • Access controls and authentication requirements
  • Secure token storage for social media connections

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access and download your personal information
  • Correct or update your account information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Disconnect social media accounts at any time
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Request data portability

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. When you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where required by law.
